# Sonos AudioClip TTS

Forked from kevinvincent/hassio-addons. We are maintaining our own version from now on — PRs go to robert-friedland/hassio-addons, not upstream.

## Releasing updates

Bump `version` in `config.json` to trigger Home Assistant update detection. HA checks the add-on repo periodically (~30 min) or manually via Settings > Add-ons > Add-on Store > Check for updates.
